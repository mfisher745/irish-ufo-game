# Chapter 2: The Bog

Region: `kilnamoe`. Before dawn, mist. Introduces the casebook, stealth and Bottle Throw.

## Screens

| ID | Name | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `c2.bog` | The bog | 60 × 34 | Turf banks, drains (not walkable), stacked turf as cover, the scorched ring in the middle. |
| `c2.farm` | Brennan's farm | 40 × 30 | Yard, sheep pens, hay shed, a dog that is asleep throughout. |
| `c2.lane` | The lane | 90 × 12 | A long straight boreen with a cattle grid at the far end. Used only for the finale. |

## Beats

1. **The ring** (`c2.explore.ring`). Casebook tutorial. Key clues: `c2.clue.ring` (scorched turf, a perfect circle, 9 metres), `c2.clue.fragment` (metal, warm, stamped MAS-4471-K), `c2.clue.tracks` (twin-wheel van tracks leaving towards Brennan's). Colour clues: a boiled frog, a single burnt welly.
2. **Bottle Throw.** A van arrives. Three men in suits get out and say they are from the Department of Agriculture to nobody in particular. Myko, by phone from the gate, points out that Andrew's pockets are full of last night's empties. Right click throws one; the noise pulls a guard to where it lands.
3. **Checkpoint, then the bog crossing** (`c2.stealth.bog`, stealth). Three `suit`s with torches sweep the bog. Turf stacks break line of sight. Follow the tyre tracks to the farm. Clean-run reward: `c2.clue.lanyard`, a dropped visitor pass reading Meridian Agri-Solutions.
4. **The yard** (`c2.puzzle.sheep`, puzzle). The tracks end at the hay shed and the yard is full of sheep. Three gates each swing between two positions. Sheep move away from Andrew and settle in whichever pen is open. Get the flock into the far pen to clear the shed door. Solvable in five gate changes.
5. **The shed** (`c2.explore.shed`). Key clue: `c2.clue.docket`, a delivery docket for "soil harmoniser, 40 units" from the Meridian depot, signed for by nobody. Pickup: a bottle of poitín behind the hay, the first one in the game. Myko advises against it and does not expect to be listened to.
6. **Two cousins** (`c2.fight.yard`, fight). Two `cousin`s have heard about last night and one `lad` has come for the look of it.
7. **Casebook conclusion.** No deduction board yet. Andrew writes the conclusion himself (the fragment, the tracks and the docket all point to the depot) and Myko's margin note agrees for the first time, with reservations.
8. **Checkpoint, then the ram** (`c2.setpiece.ram`, setpiece).

## Set piece: the ram

A Brennan ram takes exception to Andrew at the yard gate. It cannot be hurt.

- Andrew runs the length of `c2.lane`, left to right, with the ram behind. The camera leads.
- Obstacles: puddles (slow), a wheelbarrow, a gate that must be opened with E, a sleeping dog (go round).
- A thrown bottle that lands near the ram makes it stop and look for 1.5 seconds.
- A hit from the ram costs health and knocks Andrew forward, which is not entirely unhelpful.
- It ends at the cattle grid, which the ram will not cross. It stands there and looks at him.
- Myko has been sitting on the first gate the whole time.

## Sections for call-in

`c2.puzzle.sheep`, `c2.fight.yard`, `c2.setpiece.ram` (Myko says one word to the ram and it goes home).

## Closing scene

At the cattle grid. Andrew lays out the case. Myko says a depot on the edge of town that sells nothing is at least odd, and that is as far as he will go.
