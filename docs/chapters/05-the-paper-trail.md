## Beats

1. **The lobby** (`c5.explore.lobby`). The Office of Atmospheric Records takes enquiries by ticket. Andrew's number is 847. They are serving 12. Colour clue: a noticeboard with a retirement party sign-up sheet for an Agent Pruitt, three years out.
2. **Act Natural.** Myko observes that nobody has ever looked at a man in a queue. Standing still beside a marked prop makes Andrew invisible to guards. Taught on the lobby guard, to reach the Staff door.
3. **Checkpoint, then the records floor** (`c5.stealth.floor`, stealth). Six `agent`s patrol in overlapping loops. Props: two vending machines, the water cooler, a noticeboard, a queue for the photocopier. Louder footsteps at Locked matter here more than anywhere. Clean-run reward: `c5.clue.memo`, a memo from Pruitt asking, for the fourth time, for a second stapler.
4. **Files** (`c5.explore.files`). Key clues: `c5.clue.requisition` (a requisition for "replacement corn, 40 acres"), `c5.clue.busticket` (an expenses claim for a bus to somewhere redacted). Five loose sheets from one redacted file are scattered through the cubicles.
5. **Redaction puzzle** (`c5.puzzle.redacted`, puzzle). At the lightbox, choose three of the five sheets to overlay. Each copy has different words blacked out, and the right three together show one full sentence. A wrong set shows a different, useless sentence. Reward: key clue `c5.clue.site`, naming a test plot in Wendell County, Ohio.
6. **The photocopier.** Andrew copies the page for the casebook. The copier jams, and the jam alarm is the building alarm.
7. **Cubicles** (`c5.fight.floor`, fight). Agents in numbers: three waves of three `agent`s, coming through different cubicle lanes. The first fight where flanking matters.
8. **Deduction board** (`c5.deduction.ohio`, deduction). Link `c5.clue.site`, `c5.clue.requisition` and `c5.clue.busticket` to name the lead: Wendell County, Ohio.
9. **Checkpoint, then Agent Pruitt, round one** (`c5.boss.pruitt1`, boss).

## Boss: Agent Pruitt, round one

Tired and courteous. He asks them to stop, once, and means it kindly.

- **Phase 1.** He stays at range. Taser dart, and a hand crank that slides a rolling shelf to close the lane Andrew is in (floor rails light up 1 second before). Being caught between shelves costs health and pushes Andrew out.
- **Phase 2 (half health).** Two `agent`s join. Pruitt cranks two shelves at once, leaving one safe lane.
- **End.** It is five o'clock. Pruitt stops, puts on his coat and tells them he will have to write this up in the morning. The exit is unlocked.

Arena: 30 × 16 of the stacks, five lanes.

A bus station in the evening. Andrew says Ohio the way he said Boston. Myko, who has had no opinion about any place so far, goes quiet and then asks whether he heard that right.
