## Beats

1. **Arrivals** (`c4.explore.airport`). The chapter starts with a hangover: maximum health down 25% and the bright overlay on. Myko feels fine. The cure is tea, and the kiosk sells something it calls tea. It works anyway. Pickup: American light beer from a vending fridge, the first one, with the pickup text from DESIGN.md section 2.
2. **The bar** (`c4.explore.bar`). Americans explain Ireland to Andrew. Four patrons each have a short conversation, and Andrew agrees with all of it because it is quicker. Colleen-Ann is proud, wrong and useful: she knows every docker by name. Key clues: `c4.clue.roster` (a Pier Nine shift roster pinned by the dartboard), `c4.clue.patch` (the Pier Nine patch on a regular's jacket, which has the same mark as the fragment). Colour: the stout she pours, which Andrew watches settle with visible grief; a craft IPA and Myko's opinion of it.
3. **Stout Slam.** A patron asks Andrew to say something Irish. He says "no". A second patron takes this personally. Andrew puts his pint down hard and everyone near him falls over. F is taught; it costs 20 Buzz.
4. **The brawl** (`c4.fight.brawl`, fight). Six `brawler`s in three waves of two. Colleen-Ann throws Andrew a bourbon between waves and keeps a tally of breakages.
5. **Checkpoint, then the docks** (`c4.stealth.docks`, stealth). Four `docker`s on patrol and two fixed floodlights that sweep. Container rows give cover. Clean-run reward: bourbon in a lunchbox.
6. **Container puzzle** (`c4.puzzle.containers`, puzzle). Container MAS-4471 is on the top tier, out of reach. Three levers each slide one container along its rail by one slot. Line the three up as steps. Solvable in six pulls.
7. **Inside the container** (`c4.explore.container`). It is empty apart from key clue `c4.clue.seizure`: a federal notice taped to the wall. Contents removed by the Office of Atmospheric Records, Washington DC. Colour clue: packing foam with the outline of something round.
8. **Deduction board** (`c4.deduction.dc`, deduction). Link `c4.clue.seizure`, `c4.clue.roster` (a "federal pickup" marked every second Tuesday) and `c3.clue.manifest` to name the lead: the Office of Atmospheric Records.
9. **Checkpoint, then the foreman** (`c4.boss.foreman`, boss).

## Boss: the dock foreman

He does not care what is in the containers. He cares that Andrew is not in the union.

- **Phase 1.** He works the crane from the control box. A container swings across the arena on a marked path (shadow telegraph 1.2 seconds). Two `docker`s keep Andrew busy. Stout Slam next to the control box knocks him off it.
- **Phase 2.** On foot with a cargo hook: a long sweep, an overhead with a ground marker, and a pull that drags Andrew in unless he Stumbles.
- **End.** His shift ends. He says so, clocks out and leaves.

Arena: 28 × 16, open, with the crane rail along the top.
