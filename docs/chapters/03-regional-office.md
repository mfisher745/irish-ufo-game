## Beats

1. **Checkpoint, then the yard** (`c3.stealth.yard`, stealth). One night watchman (a `suit` who walks a fixed loop and stops to check his phone) and two more `suit`s by the vans. Bottle Throw is the tool. Clean-run reward: a pint of stout left on the portacabin step, origin unknown.
2. **The office** (`c3.explore.office`). Key clues: `c3.clue.map` (a wall map with one pin in Leitrim and one in Boston, and string between them), `c3.clue.brochure` (a product range with no prices and no descriptions). Colour clues: Halloran's "Regional Manager of the Year" certificate, awarded by Halloran; a visitors' book with no visitors.
3. **The Stumble.** In the warehouse aisle a drum rolls off a shelf. Andrew trips over a pallet and it misses him. Myko suggests doing that on purpose. Space is taught against three more rolling drums.
4. **Crate puzzle** (`c3.puzzle.crates`, puzzle). The manifest is in the bay office and the door is behind stacked crates. Push-only crates on an 8 × 6 grid, five crates, two fixed pillars. A reset lever sits by the entrance. Solvable in nine pushes.
5. **The manifest** (`c3.explore.bayoffice`). Key clue: `c3.clue.manifest` (forty containers a year to Pier Nine Freight, Boston; contents listed as "agricultural"). The lights come on.
6. **Night shift** (`c3.fight.bay`, fight). Four `worker`s throw boxes from the gantry side while two `lad`s in hi-vis close in. This is the lesson in using The Stumble to get through thrown objects.
7. **First deduction board** (`c3.deduction.boston`, deduction). Link `c3.clue.map`, `c3.clue.manifest` and `c2.clue.fragment` (the part number prefix matches the container prefix) to name the lead: Pier Nine Freight, Boston. The board tutorial is Myko explaining that this is just string and he has seen Andrew do it on the kitchen wall.
8. **Checkpoint, then Mr Halloran** (`c3.boss.halloran`, boss).

## Boss: Mr Halloran and his forklift

He introduces himself as regional manager, mentions the region is this building, and gets on the forklift.

- **Phase 1.** Fork charge in a straight line with a reversing-beep telegraph before each run. If the forks hit a crate stack they stick for 2.5 seconds and he can be hit from the side. He also lifts and drops a pallet as an area attack with a shadow marker.
- **Phase 2 (half health).** He carries a pallet on the forks as a shield. Andrew has to Stumble through or past the charge and hit him from behind. Charges come in pairs.
- **End.** The forklift runs out of gas. Halloran stays seated and asks whether they have an appointment.

Arena: 26 × 14 of the loading bay, four crate stacks that break after two hits each.

## Closing scene

Outside the depot. Andrew says Boston as though it settles things, and it does not, because he has €14. Myko has already booked two flights on his phone and will not discuss where the money came from. The hangover rule is live from here.
