# Chapter 7: Gift Shop

Region: `roswell`. Midday glare. Introduces aliens in disguise and Sliotar Strike. Everyone is dressed as an alien. Some of them are aliens.

From here on, about two in three of Myko's tangents are about Ohio. Roswell is compared with it and loses.

## Beats

1. **The strip** (`c7.explore.strip`). Andrew has come four thousand miles to prove aliens exist and is now in a town where saying so is a business model. Nobody is interested. Pickup: tequila, the first. Colour clue: a tourist leaflet with Andrew's bog ring on it, listed as "unverified, Europe".
2. **Who is an alien** (`c7.puzzle.tells`, puzzle). In the shop there are six figures in alien costumes. Three are tourists and three are aliens wearing alien costumes over themselves. Examine each for tells, then accuse three. The tells: no sweat in the heat, blinking sideways, standing in a perfect queue of one, counting change to the cent, reading the snow globes' small print. Tourists have human tells: sunburn, a phone, complaining. A wrong accusation embarrasses Andrew and costs nothing. Which three are aliens is fixed, not random, so the dialogue can be written to it.
3. **Gary.** The third alien is Gary, behind the till, who admits it at once. He has worked here for 19 years and likes it. He is the first person in the game to take Andrew seriously, and he is a bit bored by the subject. He gives key clue `c7.clue.payslip` (his payslip, issued by Meridian Basin Facility, Nevada).
4. **Sliotar Strike.** Myko has had a sliotar in his coat since 2009 and has never mentioned it. Hold right click to charge and hit; the ball pierces a line of enemies and comes back. Taught on a row of inflatable aliens in the yard.
5. **Checkpoint, then the stockroom** (`c7.stealth.stock`, stealth). Four `clerk`s are doing a stocktake after hours. They are precise and their patrols are perfectly regular, which is the weakness. Key clue: `c7.clue.permit` (a staff parking permit for "Gate C, service entrance"). Clean-run reward: a spice bag, inexplicably, in the staff fridge with Gary's name on it. Gary says to go ahead.
6. **The yard** (`c7.fight.yard`, fight). Three `mascot`s and three `clerk`s. The clerks line up to fire, which is what Sliotar Strike is for.
7. **The van** (`c7.explore.van`). Key clue: `c7.clue.route` (a delivery log: Roswell to Nevada every Thursday, returning empty).
8. **Deduction board** (`c7.deduction.nevada`, deduction). Link `c7.clue.payslip`, `c7.clue.permit` and `c7.clue.route` to name the lead: Meridian Basin Facility, Gate C.
9. **Checkpoint, then the three mascots** (`c7.boss.mascots`, boss).

## Boss: three "mascots" who are not mascots

Zip, Zap and Zorp, in seven-foot foam costumes, on the strip at dusk. Tourists film it and assume it is a show.

- **Zip** tumbles: a rolling body slam along a marked line, then lies on his back for 2 seconds.
- **Zap** shoots: three-bolt fans from a foam ray gun that is not foam. Bolts can be deflected.
- **Zorp** shields: stands in front of the other two with a novelty sign that blocks everything from the front.
- They keep formation. When they line up, a charged Sliotar Strike goes through all three, and that is the intended answer. When one falls, the other two speed up. When the last falls, the tourists applaud and one tips Andrew a dollar.

Arena: 30 × 14 of the strip.

## Closing scene

Outside the shop at night. Gary sees them off, wishes them luck and asks them not to mention him. Andrew now has a dollar. Myko says the roads here are narrow.
